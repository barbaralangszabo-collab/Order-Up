Order Up!

Practice how to order in a restaurant. (Level A2)
ℹ️ How to use this app (Click to open/close)

1. Learn Dishes: Pick a cuisine and a category. Click 🔊 to hear the word, or tap a card for the Hungarian translation.
2. Roleplay: Pick a guest. Build their reply by tapping the words in order, then press Check. Move a word with ◀ ▶. Learn the guest's own words and phrases on the left.
3. Gap Fill: Pick a guest. One word is missing from their sentence — tap the right word to fill the gap.
4. Memory Game: Pick a cuisine and match each food photo with its name.
🖨️ Print your own menu: Click "Print - Create Menu" to print a blank menu template — design your own restaurant's menu!
